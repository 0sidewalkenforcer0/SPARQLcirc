===== reading the result =====
OPTIONAL  ≡  (P1 AND P2)  UNION  (P1 DIFF P2)  — so every answer is a ⊕ of a matched
(⊗) term and/or an unmatched (⊖) term. On this data (Alice knows Bob,Carol; only Bob
has a city, Rome):

  y=Bob  c=Rome | ⊕((⊗t1,t6))              matched branch: knows(Alice,Bob)=t1 ⊗ city(Bob,Rome)=t6
  y=Bob         | ⊕((⊖ ⊕(⊗t1), ⊕(⊗t6)))    unmatched branch: t1 minus the (existing) city t6
  y=Carol       | ⊕((⊖ ⊕(⊗t2), ∅))         unmatched branch: t2 minus ∅ (Carol has no city)

Each ?fprovN binds a matching triple's statement id (t1..t6); ProvProd=(⊗ ...),
ProvAggSum=⊕(...), ProvDiff=(⊖ ...). The doubled ⊕(⊕(...)) is the inner per-branch
aggregation nested inside the outer final aggregation. The whole provenance is emitted
as CONCAT strings and computed by the stock engine — no engine modification.

NOTE: DIFF here is the algebra's anti-join (the negative half of OPTIONAL), NOT the
user-level SPARQL MINUS keyword. They differ when the operands share no variable: DIFF
removes such μ, MINUS keeps it (no-op). Our OPTIONAL uses DIFF (correct). The disjoint-
domain MINUS case is a separate, known guard issue — see docs/EVALUATION.md.
